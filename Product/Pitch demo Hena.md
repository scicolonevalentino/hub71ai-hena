# Hena Abu Dhabi — three-minute live demo

## One-sentence purpose

**Hena helps blind and low-vision people starting a new job in Abu Dhabi find one practical next step at a time.**

## Opening (20 seconds)

“Open Hena on your phone. You can see who it is for and the three steps: ask, get a step shaped around access, reach a verified contact. Keep your phone silent for a moment; one Hena voice will come through the room. If you are comfortable, close your eyes. Imagine arriving in Abu Dhabi for a new job. Documents, home and transport matter, but so does feeling that this city can become yours.”

## Live journey (about 2 minutes)

1. With the presenter's phone or laptop connected to the room speakers and VoiceOver off on that device, open the same live URL as the audience and activate **Talk to Hena** only there. Let the short welcome play. The audience keeps its own Hena pages silent, so many microphones and voices do not compete.
2. Ask one specific question aloud: “I want to hear Arabic music in Abu Dhabi during my first month. What can I do?” Hena recommends Bait Al Oud, names its official source and explains what must be confirmed. Stop the voice session and show the English transcript.
3. Invite everyone to open their eyes. Show **Things to do**, its four moods and 22 selectable ideas. Choose **Hear the oud**, open its official source, then show **Contact the service for this step**: Abu Dhabi Concierge, a real call link, and the question Hena prepares for a person who cannot rely on sight. Do not place a real call during the three-minute demo. Mark the action done and refresh to show progress is saved on this device.
4. Briefly open **Documents** and point to “Without relying on sight”: Hena asks HR to explain the residency process in a usable format, then shows the action and ICP source. Home and Transport use the same structure.
5. Select **Insights dashboard** in Hena's header. Point to the simulated questions Hena cannot answer yet and the suggested actions not marked done, then say: “These figures are invented for the demo. They show the kind of anonymous signal a public partner could use to add clearer answers and follow-up support.”

## Closing (20 seconds)

“Our dataset has 28 curated actions: six settlement steps and 22 first-month experiences. Every action has an official URL, a verification date and a limit. An OpenAI model inside ElevenAgents turns that evidence into a calm voice conversation, and the handoff connects each step to a verified contact. Hena Insights shows how aggregate needs and gaps could guide better support. Next, we need blind newcomers to test whether this genuinely improves their first month.”

## Demo discipline

- No slides. Keep the live URL, microphone permission and browser audio ready before the three-minute slot.
- Rehearse with the actual room audio, one live microphone, and the chosen prompt. Ask the audience to stay seated or still during the eyes-closed moment; closing eyes is optional. Have the presenter's device ready before inviting the audience in.
- A short vibration accompanies the voice activation and step save on supported Android browsers. Treat it as an optional personal detail; the iPhone web page cannot provide reliable haptics. The shared moment depends on Hena's voice and action, not vibration.
- Stay within the reviewed source actions. Do not claim a government affiliation, physical navigation or a verified accessibility guarantee for a taxi, app or venue.
- If live voice fails, type the same Arabic music question and select **Get next step**; the four source journeys and saved progress still work.
- VoiceOver behavior and product-market fit have not yet been validated with target users; describe the experience as designed for screen readers, not certified accessible.
- Show screen-reader use as a separate moment: with Hena voice off, activate VoiceOver, choose an area or dictate in the question field using the phone keyboard, and let VoiceOver read the resulting step. Do not run VoiceOver and Hena's spoken conversation at the same time during the demo.
